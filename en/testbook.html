Test Book Download

The sample book PillowBook uses to check its EPUB typesetting, free to download.

Download “枕书 · 样式测试” (PillowBook Style Test) — EPUB, about 700 KB, 24 November 2026 edition

What it is

  • A reflowable EPUB 3 with ten chapters and more than 120 short sections. Each section shows one common way EPUBs are styled: full-page backgrounds, bleeds, boxes, pictures, text styles, CSS selectors, floats, negative margins and more.
  • Every section starts with the sample, then says what the styling is meant to do, how far PillowBook gets with it today, and the CSS it uses.
  • The mark after each heading, 【已实现】【部分实现】【未实现】【backlog】, is PillowBook’s support for that styling: done, partly done, not done, and set aside for now.
  • The notes on PillowBook’s support follow the latest version in development, so a few of them only apply from the next release.

How to use it

  • On iPhone or iPad, download it, then tap “+” in PillowBook’s Library and choose “Import from Files”. You can also send it from a computer with “Wi-Fi Transfer”.
  • It is a standard EPUB, so you can open it in other readers and compare.
  • Turn through it again in another reading mode (Vertical, Scroll, Night) to see how the same styling behaves there.

Language

The book’s explanations are written in Simplified Chinese; the samples include Chinese, Japanese and English passages. If you need style samples in another language, or have met a way of styling the book doesn’t cover, please write to us.
